# Option 2


### Phase 1: Phonetic Calibration (30 Minutes)
Spanish is a phonetic language—it is spoken exactly as it is written. Your goal is to master the **5 Pure Vowels**. Unlike English, these vowels never change their sound.

* **A** (ah - like "father")
* **E** (eh - like "met")
* **I** (ee - like "see")
* **O** (oh - like "toy" but shorter)
* **U** (oo - like "boot")

**Action:** Go to YouTube and search for *"Spanish Pronunciation - The Vowels."* Repeat them aloud 50 times. If you get these right, you can read 90% of Spanish words instantly.

---

### Phase 2: Cognitive Mapping (Language Transfer) (45 Minutes)
We are going to use the **Internalized Knowledge** strategy. You already know thousands of Spanish words; you just don't know they are Spanish yet.

* **The -tion Rule:** Words ending in **-tion** in English almost always end in **-ción** in Spanish.
    * *Information* $\rightarrow$ *Información*
    * *Preparation* $\rightarrow$ *Preparación*
* **The -al Rule:** Words ending in **-al** are often identical.
    * *Animal, Capital, Hospital.*

**Action:** Listen to **Tracks 1–5 of "Language Transfer: Complete Spanish"** (Free on SoundCloud/YouTube). This will teach you how to "convert" your English brain into a Spanish one without memorization.

---

### Phase 3: The "Power 10" SRS Setup (30 Minutes)
Download **Anki** or **Quizlet**. Today, you will create your first 10 cards. These are "Functional Connectors" that allow you to build sentences immediately.

| Spanish Word | English Meaning | Note |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Es** | It is / He is / She is | The ultimate anchor. |
| **No** | No / Not | Place before the verb. |
| **Para mí** | For me | Expresses opinion/desire. |
| **Lo** | It | (e.g., *Lo quiero* - I want it). |
| **Tengo** | I have | Root: Tener. |
| **Quiero** | I want | Root: Querer. |
| **Hacer** | To do / To make | High frequency. |
| **Comer** | To eat | Basic necessity. |
| **Aquí** | Here | Spatial awareness. |
| **Ahora** | Now | Temporal awareness. |

**Goal:** Create these cards and flip through them until you hit 100% accuracy.
